# Changed: dc.sh init restores compose files from examples

With `docker-compose.yml` and its override now only tracked as `.example`
templates, `./dc.sh init` copies both `.env.example` and
`docker-compose.yml.example` (existing files are kept), and a new
`./dc.sh init:override` copies `docker-compose.override.yml.example` —
mirroring the flash-next helper.

This makes a fresh clone fully restorable with `./dc.sh init`, and matches
the usage header (also covering `init:override`).

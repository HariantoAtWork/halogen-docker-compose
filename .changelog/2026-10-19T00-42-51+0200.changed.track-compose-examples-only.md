# Changed: track compose files only as .example templates

Follow the flash-next layout: `docker-compose.yml` and
`docker-compose.override.yml` are now local-only files, with
`docker-compose.yml.example` and `docker-compose.override.yml.example`
committed as the tracked templates.

The `.example` copies hold the current contents (multi-repo download
service, env-driven serve settings, tsdproxy override). `.gitignore` now
lists both live compose files alongside `/models/` and `.env`, so
host-specific compose config stays untracked while the templates remain in
version control.
